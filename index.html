The Nominees

01 / 08
Jurassic Park
1993ActionSci-FiAdventure

A wealthy entrepreneur opens a theme park on a remote island — populated by real, cloned dinosaurs. When the park's safety systems fail, a small group of visitors must survive against creatures that have been extinct for 65 million years.

02 / 08
Spider-Man: No Way Home
2021SuperheroFantasyAction

After Spider-Man's identity is exposed, he asks Doctor Strange to make the world forget. The spell goes wrong and tears open the multiverse, pulling in villains — and heroes — from other dimensions.

03 / 08
Guardians of the Galaxy
2014ActionComedySci-Fi

A misfit crew of space outlaws — including a human adventurer, a deadly assassin, a revenge-driven warrior, and a talking raccoon and tree — must team up to stop a fanatic from destroying the galaxy.

04 / 08
The Maze Runner
2014MysteryActionSci-Fi

A teenage boy wakes up with no memory inside a vast, walled clearing. Around it lies an ever-changing maze full of deadly creatures. He and the other trapped boys must solve the maze before it kills them.

05 / 08
Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
2018AnimationSuperheroAction

Miles Morales is bitten by a radioactive spider and discovers he is not the only Spider-Man. Versions of the hero from parallel dimensions arrive in his world, and together they must stop a machine that threatens every universe.

06 / 08
Knives Out
2019MysteryComedyThriller

A famous crime novelist is found dead the morning after his 85th birthday. A sharp private detective investigates his eccentric, scheming family — and quickly discovers nothing about the case is what it first appears.

07 / 08
Interstellar
2014Sci-FiAdventureDrama

Earth is slowly dying. A former astronaut joins a small crew sent through a wormhole to find a new home for humanity among the stars — a journey that warps time itself and forces impossible choices about love and survival.

08 / 08
Monsters, Inc.
2001AnimationComedyFantasy

In a city of monsters, two best friends work at the company that powers their world by collecting children's screams. Everything changes when a small human girl accidentally wanders through a closet door into their world.
